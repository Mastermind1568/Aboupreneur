Role: You are a senior web development engineer and CRO specialist.
Task: Build a dynamic lead-generation landing page for a digital marketing 
agency serving HVAC and plumbing businesses in Edmonton, Alberta. Use 
Next.js (App Router) and Tailwind CSS.

ROUTING:
Use a dynamic route segment, not query strings.
- app/get-in-touch/page.tsx → default variant (current ad group points here)
- app/get-in-touch/[niche]/page.tsx → server-rendered, reads params.niche, 
  matches: hvac, plumbing
- Unmatched niche values fall back to default content, not a 404
- Each route sets its own <title> and meta description for that trade

Design System (Brutalist / Editorial):
- Background: bg-[#0A0A0A]
- Text: white / gray-300
- Accent: Electric Lime, text-[#B5FF3A] / bg-[#B5FF3A]
- Sharp corners, high contrast, minimalist layout

Copy matrix (H1 / Subhead per page):
Default: "Fill Your Schedule & Your Crew." / "Websites, ads, and recruiting 
help for Alberta trades businesses — one operator, one trade per city."
HVAC: "Keep Your HVAC Trucks on the Road — and Staffed." / "High-converting 
websites, Google Ads, and recruiting support for heating and cooling 
specialists."
Plumbing: "More Emergency Calls. Fewer Empty Trucks." / "Websites and 
Google Ads that bring in the calls — and help you find the crew to 
answer them."

Exclusivity line (small text near the CTA, all pages):
"We work with one HVAC company and one plumbing company per city — 
book a call to see if your city is still open."

Value props (below fold, identical across all pages):
"Fill Your Schedule", "Fill Your Crew", "Direct Access, No Overhead"

Page Layout:
Hero: 50/50 split. Left: dynamic H1, subtext, exclusivity line, email/phone 
capture form, high-contrast lime submit button. Right: dynamically injected 
visual asset per niche.

Conversion tracking:
Form submit fires consulting_form_submitted with a niche event parameter 
matching the current route ("default", "hvac", "plumbing"). Include niche 
as a hidden form field so it appears in the email/CRM notification.

Do not build beyond these 3 pages, and do not add electrical, roofing, 
renovation, or a [city] segment yet — those come in a later phase.
